Frequently Asked Questions

Why are some cards empty?

Longview only shows data that is already in the Health app, mostly recorded by Apple Watch. A card stays empty when there is no data for it yet, or when Longview isn't allowed to read that type. Longview never fills in or estimates numbers. Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Longview and make sure the types you want are turned on.

Longview says it only read the past 30 days. How do I share my full history?

When you allowed access, iOS may have been set to share only the past 30 days. Open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Longview, and change what you share to All recorded and future data. Then open Longview again.

What does the Today card mean?

It compares today's HRV, your latest resting heart rate, and last night's sleep with your own average over the previous 28 days. Each line says whether it is above, close to, or below your usual. A difference of more than 10% counts as above or below. It needs at least 7 days of history to compare. It is a plain comparison, not a score or a health assessment.

Why don't I see my Activity rings?

Activity rings come from Apple Watch. If you don't wear Apple Watch, or today's summary hasn't synced to your iPhone yet, the card stays empty.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Most charts use data that Apple Watch saves to Health, such as heart rate, HRV and sleep. Without Apple Watch, Longview can still show any of these that another app or device has saved to Health.

Where is my data stored?

Your Health data stays in the Health app on your iPhone. Longview reads it on your device and never uploads it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Longview a medical app?

No. Longview is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Talk to a doctor about any health concerns.
