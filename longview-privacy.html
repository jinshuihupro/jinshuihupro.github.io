Privacy Policy

Longview - HRV Trends ("Longview") is provided by jinshuihu. Effective November 11, 2026.

Longview is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.

Health Data Stays on Your iPhone

With your permission, Longview reads data from Apple Health through HealthKit: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (SDNN), blood oxygen, respiratory rate, sleep, workouts, active energy, activity summaries, and date of birth. You choose which of these to allow, and you can change that at any time.

Longview uses this data only to draw charts, trends and comparisons on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, never stored in iCloud by Longview, and we cannot see it. Longview does not keep its own copy of your Health data; it reads from Health each time the dashboard opens.

Read-Only

Longview only reads from Health. It never writes, changes or deletes your Health data.

No Network, Accounts, Analytics or Ads

Longview makes no network requests. It has no account or sign-in, collects no analytics or crash reports, uses no advertising identifiers, contains no ads and no third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

No Sharing or Selling

We do not collect your data, so we never share it, sell it, or use it for advertising, marketing or data mining. Health data is never used for any purpose other than showing you your own charts.

Your Control

  • Turn off any data type at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Longview.
  • Deleting Longview removes the app and its settings from your iPhone. It does not affect your data in Health.

Children

Longview is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above. If a future version ever adds anonymous crash reporting, we will update this policy first, and your Health data will still never leave your device.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact jinshuihu.app@gmail.com.
