Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Click Cast (“the app”), provided by jinshuihu, handles information when you use the app. We do not require you to create an account to use core remote-control features.

How the App Works

Click Cast discovers and controls compatible smart TVs on your local Wi-Fi network, and may offer casting, keyboard, touchpad, quick launch, Premium purchases, ads for free users, and an optional Apple Watch companion.

Information We Process

  • Local network / device discovery data needed to find and communicate with TVs on the same Wi-Fi network (processed on your local network)
  • Photos or videos you select only when you choose media to cast; transfer happens over your local network and is not uploaded to Click Cast servers for storage
  • Purchase / subscription status related to Premium entitlements (billing is handled by Apple; we may receive entitlement status via Apple / purchase infrastructure such as RevenueCat to unlock features)
  • Diagnostic and crash data used to improve stability (for example via crash reporting services)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) and ad interaction data if ads are shown to free users, processed by our advertising partners according to their policies and platform rules
  • IP address, and the approximate country-level location derived from it, used by advertising partners to deliver ads, cap how often you see them, prevent fraud, and determine which privacy rules apply to you
  • Support emails you voluntarily send us, used only to troubleshoot and reply
  • Feature-use analytics, such as control counts, session duration, feature categories, and pairing or casting outcomes, help us understand usage and improve the app. These events do not include TV names, IP or MAC addresses, pairing PINs, or the text you enter. Analytics is provided by Firebase Analytics.

How Information Is Used

  • To provide remote control, casting, and related app features
  • To unlock Premium features and restore purchases
  • To show ads to free users where enabled
  • To investigate crashes and improve reliability
  • To respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data may be processed by third-party providers that help operate the app, such as:

  • Apple (App Store billing, subscriptions, and platform services)
  • Purchase entitlement infrastructure (for example RevenueCat)
  • Crash reporting / analytics providers
  • Advertising providers, for free users when ads are enabled: Yandex Mobile Ads and Appodeal (including the demand partners Appodeal mediates on our behalf)
  • From version 1.2.1, Appodeal is the only advertising platform, with mediation partners including BidMachine, AppLovin, Unity Ads, and Vungle. Earlier app versions may still use Yandex Mobile Ads.

These providers process data according to their own policies and only as needed to provide their services.

Advertising and Tracking

Click Cast shows ads to users on the free tier. Our advertising partners may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) and IP address to select and measure ads. Under Apple’s rules this counts as tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

We therefore ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown, but they are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the EEA, the UK, and other regions with equivalent rules, a consent management platform built into our advertising SDK asks for your consent before personalised advertising data is processed, and records your choice. Purchasing Premium removes all ads.

Data Retention

Local control and casting traffic stays on your network. Entitlement and diagnostic data retained by us or our providers is kept only as needed to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, or resolve issues, then deleted or anonymized where practical.

Your Choices

  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in App Store account settings
  • You can contact us to ask privacy-related questions
  • You can allow or refuse tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and change your answer at any time
  • You can remove all ads by purchasing Premium
