Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

One Day Closer is provided by jinshuihu.

How the App Works

One Day Closer shows how long until you retire, over a full-screen landscape photo. You set a retirement date, and the app counts the days, optionally counting workdays only and taking off your vacation days. You can also add widgets, set a daily reminder, and share a card.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • The retirement date you set (and, if you entered them in an earlier version, your birth date and work-start date)
  • Your appearance and counting settings, such as photo, font, color, workdays only, vacation days, and reminder time
  • A copy of a personal photo, if you choose one as your background

This information is stored only on your device and shared with the app's own widget through a private app group. It is not uploaded to us, there is no account or sign-in, and we cannot see it.

Your Photos

If you use your own photo as a background, you pick it with the iOS photo picker. The app does not ask for access to your photo library and sees only the photo you pick. A copy of that photo is kept only on your device, for the home screen and widgets. It is never uploaded. Picking another photo of your own replaces it, and Clear my data in Settings removes it.

When you save a share card, the app asks for permission to add photos only. It saves the card to your library and does not read your other photos. If you decline, you can still share the card in other ways.

Daily Reminder

The daily reminder is a local notification created on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on. The reminder text is generated on your device and nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Built-in Photos

The landscape photos included in the app come from Unsplash and are used under the Unsplash License. The photographers and locations are listed in the app under About → Photo locations. These photos ship with the app, and viewing them does not make any network request.

Diagnostic and Crash Data

The released app uses a third-party crash reporting service (Sentry) to collect crash reports and basic diagnostic information (such as app version, device model, and operating system) so we can fix problems and improve stability. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you.

Product Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated product use: whether onboarding finished, whether a goal save succeeded, the result of a share, and which time format was selected. These events contain only fixed labels. They do not include your birth date, retirement date, photos, or any text you enter. Firebase Analytics here does not use an advertising identifier and is not used to track you across other companies' apps.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Diagnostic data is processed by the crash reporting provider. Product interaction events are processed by Firebase Analytics. All network transport uses standard HTTPS/TLS.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact jinshuihu.app@gmail.com.
