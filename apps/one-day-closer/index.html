App Overview

One Day Closer

One Day Closer opens to a full-screen landscape and how long until you retire. Every day you open it, you are one day closer. Choose your preferred language below.

App Store

What's in One Day Closer

  • A full-screen landscape home. Swipe for days, years and months, minutes and seconds.
  • aA: change the photo, font and color, or use your own photo.
  • Big Countdown: months, weeks, days and seconds at once, in portrait or landscape.
  • Count workdays only, and take off your vacation days.
  • A daily reminder at the time you choose. You can turn it off any time.
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets with your photo as the background.
  • Share this page, a designed card or a single line, or save the card to Photos.

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases. Your date and your photo stay on this iPhone. Built-in photos are from Unsplash.
