App Overview

Longview

Longview turns the heart rate, HRV, sleep and activity data your Apple Watch saves to Health into one dashboard on your iPhone, and shows how today compares with your own usual.

What's in Longview

  • Today: your HRV, resting heart rate and last night's sleep, each compared with your previous 28 days.
  • Today's heart rate as a full-day chart, with min, average and max.
  • The last 7 days, and today against your usual range for the same hours.
  • HRV, resting heart rate, sleeping heart rate, blood oxygen and respiratory rate.
  • Workouts, Activity rings, sleep stages and weekly cardio points.
  • Tap a card to see 7, 30 or 90 days with the average, low and high.

Private by design

Longview reads from Health and never writes to it. Everything is calculated on your iPhone. No internet connection, no account, no ads, no analytics.
