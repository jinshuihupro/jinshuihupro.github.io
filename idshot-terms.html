What IDShot Does

IDShot checks technical specifications only. Final acceptance is decided by the U.S. Department of State.

IDShot checks and fixes the technical file requirements for the DS-160 / CEAC photo upload: size, pixel dimensions, format, file size and color. It only crops, resizes, re-encodes and converts color. It never changes your appearance or background, because official rules do not allow altered photos.

Whether a photo is accepted also depends on things IDShot cannot guarantee, such as expression, lighting, glasses, background and how recently the photo was taken. IDShot does not promise that your photo or application will be accepted.
